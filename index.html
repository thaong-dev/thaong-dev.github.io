Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Thaong Dev ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications handle permissions and user data.

1. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Our applications request access to your device's camera strictly for real-time barcode scanning, QR code decoding, and optical recognition functionality.

  • All video frames and camera images are processed locally in real-time within your device's memory (RAM).
  • We do not record, store, or transmit your camera feed or personal photos to any external servers or third parties.

2. Local Storage & Data Processing

Any scanned barcode records, text data, or exported files (such as CSV or XLSX files) generated within our apps are stored locally on your device under your direct control. We do not collect or harvest your business or personal scan logs.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Our core scanning tools are designed to operate 100% offline.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
