Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Mobile Computing Solutions (Thaong Dev) ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android applications—including QR Scanner (dev.thaong.qrscanner), PDF Editor: Fast PDF to Excel (dev.thaong.pdfeditor), and Cam 365: Camera, Video & Audio (dev.thaong.cam365)—handle device permissions, local files, advertising, and in-app purchases.

Offline-First Principle: All core features—scanning barcodes, reading/converting/comparing PDF documents, capturing photos and videos, editing media, playing local files, and recording audio—operate locally on your device. We do not require user accounts and we never upload your personal files, photos, documents, or recordings to our servers.

1. Device Permissions We Use

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used in QR Scanner for real-time barcode/QR decoding, in PDF Editor for document capture, and in Cam 365 for taking photos and recording videos. Camera frames are processed locally in real time and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Microphone / Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively in Cam 365 to capture audio during video recording and offline voice memo recording. Audio streams are processed locally (including on-device noise suppression) and saved directly to your device storage.
  • Local Files & Media Storage (MediaStore / Storage Access Framework): Used across all three apps to open, edit, play, and export user-selected files (such as PDFs, Excel/CSV spreadsheets, photos, videos, and audio tracks). You retain full ownership and control over every file stored on your device.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi Access (LAN Sync): Used optionally when you connect your mobile app to our companion Desktop PC Hub over your private local network (Wi-Fi/LAN) for data transfer or desktop license activation. No data is routed through cloud servers.

2. Advertising & Advertising ID (Free Versions)

Free versions of our applications display advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver ads, measure ad performance, and prevent invalid traffic or fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information, including your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, coarse location, and app interaction/diagnostic data.

  • Purpose of collection by Google AdMob: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, and Fraud prevention, security, and compliance.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), or upgrade to the Pro Lifetime edition to permanently disable all advertisements.
  • Learn more about how Google handles data in ads at Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. In-App Purchases & Billing Data

Our applications offer optional one-time in-app purchases (such as pro_lifetime for mobile unlocks and pc_hub for desktop companion slots) processed strictly through Google Play Billing.

  • We do not collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or payment credentials.
  • Purchase tokens, order IDs, and local purchase status are used solely to verify entitlements, restore your Pro upgrade on Android, and generate offline cryptographic licenses for connected Desktop PC Hub devices.

4. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell your personal data. Aside from data automatically processed by trusted platform SDKs required for app monetization and billing (Google AdMob and Google Play Billing), we do not share your documents, media, scan logs, or personal information with any third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because our apps do not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, all app-generated data (scan history, exported Excel/PDF files, edited photos/videos, and recordings) resides exclusively on your device. You may delete any file at any time inside the app or via your device's file manager, and uninstalling the app removes its private local app data.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are general productivity and media utilities intended for general audiences and are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Updates take effect immediately upon posting on this page with an updated effective date.
